# SEO/GEO content and growth audit

Date: 2026-10-23. Site: https://rating.watch. Scope: source content, the existing prerendered content sample, the three unpublished film drafts, and direct primary-source checks. This is a browser-extension product with an associated ratings reference hub. No Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, analytics account or paid keyword dataset was available to this audit.

Read the SaaS strategy template and E-E-A-T reference, but sized recommendations to a small, independently maintained extension rather than a subscription software sales funnel. ## Findings and actions

1. **High: a ratings-definition gap between existing guides.** The site already has installation, IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, viewing-choice, comparison and research coverage. Netflix's own thumbs, recommendation cues and maturity classifications need a separate answer that makes their different jobs clear. The root task is building `/how-to/netflix-ratings-explained`; this task added contextual links from all three existing how-to pages. This is one differentiated guide, rather than copies targeting different devices or keyword spellings.
2. **High before publication: the three film drafts passed mechanical checks while containing misleading interpretation.** KPop's Metascore was incorrectly described as closer to its Rotten Tomatoes percentage; it is closer to scaled IMDb. Drafts confused chart hours with people pressing play, joined different reporting windows, inferred viewer motives from aggregates, and used incomparable metrics to describe relative enjoyment. These issues were revised in all three JSON drafts. Their summaries now distinguish the arithmetic difference from any measured difference in enjoyment.
3. **Release gate: the film drafts remain unpublished.** They have documented preparatory data checks and matching Rotten Tomatoes checks; `published` remains `null`. The existing pilot specification requires an independent second fact-check, owner scores of at least 4.0 on average with none below 3, and the specified pilot archetypes. Those requirements have not been established by this task. They remain at `status: review`: a green lint run or the same agent's data pass is not independent editorial approval. The current three drafts contain two critics-higher films and one audience-higher film, rather than the specification's requested agreed-archetype pilot.
4. **Medium: strengthen evidence before expanding programmatic pages.** The hub exposes 100 dated film records, a selection rule, excluded matches, a downloadable dataset, an editorial method and correction contact. Its generated article registry currently contains no published film analyses. This protects the sitemap from unfinished pages. `data/blog/source/doubtful.json` also records unresolved older-film identity candidates; those require the existing identity process before an affected article can be checked. Keep the per-film gates and gradual rollout.
5. **Medium: compare products by their actual job.** `/compare/alternatives` already distinguishes in-Netflix overlays from independent streaming guides and links to sources beside claims. JustWatch's directly inspected homepage describes cross-service discovery and IMDb filters; it does not establish the behavior of every competing extension. Preserve explicit last-verified dates and unknowns, rather than invent feature/price negatives or publish separate near-identical competitor pages.
6. **Low: old strategy documents contain obsolete operating assumptions.** June strategy/calendar/competitor documents still describe the previous domain, open-source licensing, a pending store listing and outdated schema advice. The current site uses Rating Watch, rating.watch, an existing Chrome Web Store listing and restricted licensing. Treat this report and current application data as the operational baseline, and revise those historical documents before reusing their copy or forecasts.

## Draft revision and fact-check record

All three existing JSON files were revised as a writer pass, then checked against the committed OMDb/Netflix fields and the directly opened Rotten Tomatoes title pages. No outside production or awards claims were added. Standard generated data sources include OMDb, IMDb, Netflix Top 10 and the verified Rotten Tomatoes page; the authored `sources` arrays remain empty. No claim that the maker watched or personally reviewed a film was added.

- **Carry-On:** 734 authored prose words. Removed predictions of a good evening, unsupported inference about audience behavior, and a misleading connection between its viewing window and chart-week count. Retained the numerical Metascore proximity as a qualified interpretation. Added the difference between numeric scale and measurement. Rotten Tomatoes check: **87%, 107 critic reviews**, matching OMDb's 87%; title, year and director match. [Primary title page](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/carry_on).
- **KPop Demon Hunters:** 720 authored prose words. Corrected the Metascore-proximity claim; removed the suggestion that awards establish critic approval and that its musical/action mix explains viewer response; described weekly-summed chart hours without claiming unique viewers. Runtime is explicitly the dated OMDb record (95 minutes), not a current runtime verification; the inspected Rotten Tomatoes listing gives 99 minutes. Rotten Tomatoes check: **92%, 107 critic reviews**, matching OMDb's 92%; title, year and both directors match. [Primary title page](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/kpop_demon_hunters).
- **Red Notice:** 721 authored prose words. Removed unsupported casual-viewer demographic/motive inference and claimed personal suitability; clarified that the Most Popular initial-release-window hours differ from accumulated chart weeks and do not count unique people. Rotten Tomatoes check: **36%, 174 critic reviews**, matching OMDb's 36%; title, year and director match. [Primary title page](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/red_notice).

Each preparatory RT check is dated 2026-10-23 with outcome `match`. All files remain `status: review`, with `factChecker` and `factCheckedOn` null because the revising agent is not an independent checker. `review.cuts` remains empty; the writer revisions are documented above rather than represented as an independent fact-check. They must not be used to declare the original pilot's cut-rate criterion satisfied. A fresh independent reviewer and the owner's pilot assessment remain outstanding.

Verification: `npm run blog:check -- --all` passes schema, tokens, copy, plot overlap, lengths, links and similarity for all three review drafts. `npx jest tests/blog --runInBand`: **21 suites, 648 tests pass**. ## Thirty-day traffic plan

1. **Days 1–3: deploy and measure the foundation.** Release the verified changes, verify the live guide, sitemap, canonical and crawl accessibility, then inspect Search Console/Bing data. Record actual organic landing-page clicks/impressions, indexed canonical URLs, and store-link clicks where existing analytics consent permits. No numeric traffic baseline is inferred from a new domain or search result absence.
2. **Days 4–7: finish the pilot review.** Have an independent reviewer check every pilot claim, resolve the required agreed-archetype selection and obtain the owner's quality scores. Publish only the approved pilot. Use the existing registry, sitemap, hub, comparison/research inbound links and dated source disclosures. Do not publish the other 97 simply to expand indexable URL count.
3. **Days 8–14: use observed query demand.** Compare query families for extension installation, IMDb/Rotten Tomatoes overlays and Netflix rating meanings. Fix a page whose impressions reveal an unanswered task before introducing a separate page. Avoid duplicating the IMDb-vs-Rotten-Tomatoes definition across title pages; link to the existing explanation.
4. **Days 15–30: assess reach and quality before scaling.** Inspect pilot indexing and actual landing-page usefulness, including support reports of wrong ratings. The existing specification requires at least 14 days and its Search Console gate before another batch. Prepare a truthful launch submission or data-study pitch for owner-approved publication; use the current Chrome Web Store listing, real maker identity and reproducible dataset. This task did not send outreach or create third-party posts.

Success is evaluated by observed search clicks, relevant query impressions, canonical indexing and useful visits reaching the store, not by a promised traffic multiplier, an internal audit score or number of pages published. A small evergreen backlog and periodic checks fit the existing maintenance constraint better than a new perpetual posting schedule.

## Primary guidance consulted

- [Netflix: how to rate TV shows and movies](https://help.netflix.com/en/node/9898): current thumbs choices inform recommendations; some old devices can still display stars. No universal current Match-percentage UI was inferred.
- [JustWatch US homepage](https://www.justwatch.com/us): directly inspected for its own streaming-guide purpose and IMDb filtering description; no unsupported competitor feature conclusion was drawn.
